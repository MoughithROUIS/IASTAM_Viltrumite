How to read this page. The animation above replays one recorded example (one message, one random draw of failures) to show the decision logic. It is not statistics. Flooding is drawn in simplified form, and the controller re-evaluates R(t) at every step here, whereas in the experiments the mode is chosen once per message at injection. The table and the chart are the statistics: mean ± std over 30 independent seeds, same traffic for all strategies within a seed. Reading the chart: up to 40% extra failures CGR-lite and the hybrid are statistically indistinguishable. Between 50% and 70% the hybrid is clearly worse than CGR-lite (its threshold θ = 0.5 switches to flooding too early). They are equal at 80%. Only at 90% does the hybrid beat CGR-lite (40% vs 27%), tracking Epidemic; at 100% nothing can be delivered. The 50–100% range was added after the initial 0–40% runs.
